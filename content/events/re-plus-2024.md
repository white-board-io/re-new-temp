Today, the world’s potential to produce renewable electricity is higher than ever. The year 2023 witnessed a significant boom in clean energy, with [50%¹](https://www.iea.org/reports/renewables-2023/executive-summary) more renewables integrated into the energy system worldwide, reaching a total of [510 GW¹](https://www.iea.org/reports/renewables-2023/executive-summary).

As the global community is moving towards developing clean energy systems, free from fossils, and committed to boosting clean energy, we at ReNew had the honour and privilege to participate in RE+ 2024 from September 9-12 in Anaheim, CA. RE+ 2024 brought together some of the brightest minds in renewable energy, offering the ReNew team a unique opportunity to network, gain insights, and discuss the industry’s future.

This year's theme was focused on advancing clean energy innovation, collaboration, and policy alignment across renewable energy sectors and the event hosted over [1300 exhibitors²](https://www.iea.org/reports/renewables-2023/executive-summary), and [40,000 clean energy professionals²](https://www.re-plus.com/news/re-24-features/), showcasing their innovative contributions to the renewable space.

### The US Renewable Energy Market and our takeaways from RE+ 2024

It is undeniable that the US's solar energy ecosystem is undergoing a massive transformation at the moment and manufacturing output from enterprises in the US is expected to grow by [2.5%¹](https://think.ing.com/articles/us-manufacturing-outlook-better-times-are-coming/#:~:text=One%20way%20this%20could%20happen,that%202007%20peak%20level%20today.). Due to trade barriers between China and the US, India is uniquely positioned for the US market with its competitive offerings from the solar manufacturing industry.

As of Q3 2023, India-US solar exports rose [246%⁴](https://www.mercomindia.com/indian-solar-exports-surge-q3-2023) compared to Q3 2022, with the US generating [$543.8 million⁴](https://www.mercomindia.com/indian-solar-exports-surge-q3-2023) in business. India’s solar panel production capacity reached 68 GW as of March 2024; the FY 2023-24 exports were valued at [$1,939.92⁵](https://www.pv-magazine-india.com/2024/05/27/indias-solar-module-exports-hit-1-97-billion-in-fiscal-2023-24/#:~:text=India's%20solar%20module%20exports%20hit%20$1%2C969.13%20million%20in%20fiscal%202023,to%20USA%20totalled%20$1%2C939.92%20million) million for solar modules.

Among Indian solar cell and module manufacturers, ReNew’s 2.5 GW cell manufacturing facility in Dholera, Gujarat, and 6.4 GW solar module manufacturing capacity in Jaipur and Dholera strengthen its competitive position in the market, supporting both India’s renewable energy goals and meeting domestic demand efficiently.

At the event, we took a deep dive into what these figures mean for the overall renewable energy ecosystem and how we can contribute to the escalation that is sure to define the next decade of the industry. We came across a lot of potential buyers and stakeholders sharing our vision for the future and are willing to help catapult the state of solar into a brighter tomorrow.
