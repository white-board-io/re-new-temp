Gift Warmth: ReNew Power to Distribute 300,000
Blankets Across 10 States in India

●         
ReNew Power to distribute 300,000 blankets this year, a record that surpasses all its previous
editions through the Gift Warmth
campaign

●         
The campaign will be active across
10 states where local authorities along with ReNew Power employees will partake in the distribution drive

 

**Gurugram, India, December 21, 2022:** ReNew Power, India’s leading
renewable energy company, launched the 8th edition of its
‘Gift Warmth’ campaign. Through the initiative, the company will distribute 300,000 blankets across 10 states
including Delhi NCR, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh,
Maharashtra, Uttarakhand, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat,
Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Karnataka. The
ambitious drive flags off in December 2022 and will continue all through
January 2023.

 

This year’s campaign
target is a significant increase
from its previous
edition, when the company distributed around 220,000 blankets.
Gift Warmth was initiated in 2015 to help the disenfranchised,
homeless populace in India battle the harsh winters. The distribution process will be undertaken at ReNew Power’s wind
and solar plants where ReNew employees will collaborate
with district officials who will identify the beneficiaries covering the
targeted districts**.** The campaign will be flagged off
through a ceremonial launch at the district level followed by distribution at tehsil and village level
around the carefully chosen locations. The drive directly helps small scale businesses from whom the
blankets have been sourced thereby helping the
local economy. The blankets will be distributed during night drives along with donations to shelters by the
company’s employees.

 

Sharing her enthusiasm surrounding the campaign,
**Vaishali Nigam Sinha, Chief Sustainability Officer, ReNew Power** said, “Giving back to communities in need has always been a goal for us at ReNew and we are proud
to usher in the eighth,
successful edition of Gift Warmth.
Winters are harsh in certain
regions of our country affecting
a multitude of people each year who hail from the weakest sections of our society. We need
to realise that this is a direct effect of climate change where we are witnessing shorter but severe
winters. We are proud of our employees who have passionately engaged with this initiative and will work on
ground with local authorities to help as many
people as they can. In the upcoming editions, we further plan to scale our
distribution numbers and encourage
other corporations to undertake similar campaigns and lend a helping hand to our fellow
citizens.”

 

Through Gift Warmth, ReNew Power aims to reach 1 million people by
2024 and is inviting contributions
from other corporates and institutions willing to participate in the drive
through donations at [www.renewfoundation.in.](http://www.renewfoundation.in/)
