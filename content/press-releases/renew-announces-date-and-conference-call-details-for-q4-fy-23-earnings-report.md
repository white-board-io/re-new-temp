plc ("ReNew") (NASDAQ: RNW, RNWWW), India's leading renewable energy company, today announced it will

Nasdaq on June 6, 2023.

**About ReNew**

ReNew is a leading decarbonisation solutions company listed on Nasdaq (Nasdaq: RNW, RNWWW).
