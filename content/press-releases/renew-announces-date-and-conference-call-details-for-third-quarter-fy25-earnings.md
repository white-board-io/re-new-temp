RNW, RNWWW), India's leading renewable energy company, today announced it will
issue its third quarter (October

18, 2025 (EST).

**About
ReNew**

ReNew is a leading decarbonization solutions company
listed on Nasdaq (Nasdaq: RNW, RNWWW). ReNew's clean energy portfolio of ~17.4
