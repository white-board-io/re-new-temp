ReNew Power appoints Ajay K Goel as President Solar & Chief New Businesses 
Gurgaon, Haryana May 19, 2015 – ReNew Power Ventures Pvt. Ltd., one of India’s largest clean energy 
companies has appointed Ajay K Goel as  President Solar & Chief  New Businesses. In his current role, 
Ajay will be responsible for building new businesses on the distributed side of the solar sector focusing 
on commercial, industrial and residential segments. This will entail  creating end to end innovative 
solutions around multiple products, techn ologies and business models such as , rooftop solar,  energy 
efficiency, energy storage, among others.  
Prior to joining ReNew Power , Ajay was the CEO at  Tata Power Solar, where he revived their  cell & 
module manufacturing business as well as moved the company downstream  in both central and 
distributed generation. Earlier, he spent 20 years in the United States gaining in -depth experience at 
leading high-tech and clean -tech companies such as SunEdison, Brightsource Energy and McKinsey  
where he helped them with strategy, new investments, product development and streamlining 
operations. 
Commenting on the appointment Sumant Sinha, Chairman and CEO, ReNew Power  said, “As a 
company, ReNew Power is committed to providing value by innovating and expand ing to new verticals 
that suit the India market. I am delighted that an industry veteran of the stature of Ajay joins us at this 
crucial juncture to drive some of the growth initiatives that will enable us to cement our position as the 
leading clean energy  solutions provider in India. It further re -affirms our commitment to meeting the 
ever-expanding energy needs of the country.” 
Commenting on his appointment Ajay said, "I am delighted to join ReNew Power, the leading renewable 
IPP in India. I have tremendous respect for the exponential growth that ReNew Power has achieved in a 
short span of time under the outstanding leadership of Sumant and I look forward to helping ReNew 
Power become the first name in Solar in India." 
Ajay is an Electrical and Computer Software Engineer from Indian Institute of Technology, New Delhi and 
holds an MBA from the University Of Chicago Booth School Of Business.
