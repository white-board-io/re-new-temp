ReNew Power bags the highest subsidy allocation from SECI for rooftop solar projects 
o ReNew Power has been allocated 49 MW under SECI’s rooftop solar mega tender of 
500 MW 
o Approx. 130 bidders participated and ReNew Power won 10% of the total bid capacity 
o The solar rooftop projects will be targeted towards institutional as well as residential 
sector installations 
 
Gurgaon, India, November 24, 2016: ReNew Power Ventures Private Limited, India’s leading renewable 
energy development company, has emerg ed as the organization with the highest allocation under the 
mega tender of 500 megawatts of rooftop solar projects floated by Solar Energy Corporation of India 
Limited (SECI)  – the designated implementing agency for this scheme , providing subsidy on roof top 
projects across various Indian states. 
The tender is targeted towards institutional (schools, colleges, universities etc.) and residential sector s, 
aiming to promote rooftop solar setups in these sectors. Approx. 130 bidders participated in the bid out 
of which ReNew Power was allocated at a tenth of the entire bid capacity at approx. 49 MW.  
Expressing views on this development, Mr. Sumant Sinha, Chairman and CEO, ReNew Power  
mentioned, “It is really encouraging to see the go vernment taking active measures to promote usage of 
renewables in India, especially solar! We always aim to walk hand in hand with the government in their 
efforts and make a prominent mark in the development of this industry.” 
Mr. Prabhat Kumar Mishra, Hea d Distributed Solar & Offtake, ReNew Power  also commented, saying, 
“We are currently riding a growth wave  and supplying renewable energy to more than fifty large 
industrial and commercial consumers across the country. This scheme will give a significant thrust to our 
business, especially to the distributed solar segment , which is now becoming a key focus area for the 
organization.” 
Having an existing pipeline of approx. 100 MW under the distributed solar segment, ReNew Power plans 
to take this segment to ne w heights under the SECI scheme with special focus on educational institutes 
across the country.
