ReNew Power organizes first ever national Green Quiz 
Teams from FMS, Delhi and TCS and Sai Mitra emerge victorious at grand finale 
Aimed at raising awareness on environment and sustainability, initiative witnessed participation 
from more than 100 business schools and 50 corporates 
November 18, 2017 – Gurgaon: ReNew Power Ventures Pvt. Ltd. (“ReNew Power”), India’s leading 
renewable energy company, today organized the first edition of  ReNew Green Quiz – an annual 
event aimed at raising awareness on issues around the environment, sustainability and renewable 
energy. Over the past few weeks, the national level quiz – the grand finale of which was held today 
at the Excelsior American School Gurugram – saw enthusiastic participation from more than 2,000 
students representing leading business schools; and more than 50 companies from around the 
country. 
The quiz was conducted in two tracks – one for business schools and the other for corporates – by 
renowned quizmaster, Avinash Mudaliar. Each track had representati on from eight teams. The 
business school track had teams from elite institutions like XLRI, FMS, IIM -Calcutta, IIM-Kashipur, 
IIM-Nagpur, MDI-Gurgaon, IIM-Indore, IIT-Bombay pit their quizzing skills against each other. The 
corporate track saw tough competi tion between employees of organizations such Mondelez, 
Wipro, EY, Value Research, ESPN, TCS, Frost & Sullivan, RateGain Travel Solutions, Lava 
International, YES Bank and Citibank.  
Team Peter’s Fortress from FMS, Delhi and Team  Ignorance is Bliss  from TCS  and Sai Mitra 
emerged as the final winners of the B -School and Corporate tracks. There were special 
commendations for ReNew Power’s employees that had won the employee segment of the quiz 
conducted earlier, and prizes for the audience as well. 
Addressing participants at the end of an exciting evening,  Mr Sumant Sinha, Chairman and Chief 
Executive Officer, ReNew Power said, “In view of the socio -economic challenges posed by climate 
change, especially the looming threat of rising urban air pollution in India , ReNew Green Quiz has 
special significance. The quiz was aimed at creating an interactive, collaborative platform for bright 
young minds to think and look for answers to the environmental and sustainability challenges of 
today. In this, we hope our initiative has been successful.” 
About ReNew Power 
ReNew Power Ventures Pvt. Ltd, an Independent Power Producer (IPP), is committed to leading 
change in the country’s current energy portfolio by delivering cleaner and smarter energy choices 
thereby, reducing Ind ia’s carbon footprint. ReNew Power’s mission is to play a pivotal role in 
meeting India’s growing energy needs in an efficient, sustainable and socially responsible manner. 
The company creates value through reliable and efficient generation of non -conventional power 
through innovation in solar and wind power solutions. ReNew Power has over 3,500 MW of

commissioned and under construction clean energy capacity across 16 states in the country. As a 
socially responsible company, ReNew Power impacts the lives of 1.32 lakh people in 92 villages in 
and around its project sites across various states in India.
