ReNew Power provides 50 Oxygen Concentrators to UP 
Government to help in its fight against COVID 
Gorakhpur, India, June 09, 2021: ReNew Power Private Limited (“ReNew Power” or the “Company”), 
India’s leading renewable energy company today handed over 50 Oxygen concentrators to Shri K. 
Vijayendra Pandian, IAS, the District Magistrate of Gorakhpur, to assist the State Administration in its 
fight against COVID. The units were handed over by senior officials from ReNew Power.  
The concentrators are part of ReNew Power’s CSR initiative to help government hospitals combat 
COVID in various districts of the country. Besides supplying oxygen concentrators to the government 
hospitals, ReNew Power is also supplementing the efforts of various State Governments through 
donation of extra beds, ambulance support and distribution of PPE kits to frontline workers in 
hospitals.  
Speaking about the Initiative, Ms Vaishali Nigam Sinha, Chief Sustainability Officer, ReNew Power 
and Chair, ReNew Foundation said, “ReNew Power is committed to extending its support across the 
country to fight the pandemic. We are honoured to be able to support the Uttar Pradesh 
Government in its efforts to fight the pandemic, under the able leadership of the Hon’ble CM Shri 
Yogi Adityanath ji.  We will continue to ensure that necessary support is available and supplied on 
priority to the U.P State Administration.” 
ReNew Power’s engagement in UP, dates to 2016 when responding to Hon’ble Prime Minister Shri 
Narendra Modi Ji’s call, the company adopted Paniyara village in Varanasi, with a vision to develop it 
as a “Model village” in line with the philosophy of “Sansad Adarsh Gram Yojana”, ensuring all 
households have access to electricity. 
The Company has since then installed community solar grids of 51 KW which benefits 300 homes, 8 
schools and over 1000 children with continuous access to electricity. ReNew also runs basic literacy 
programs which benefit 260 children and farmer programs where over 1000 farmers undergo 
training to enhance productivity of their farms.  
During the first wave of COVID -19 in India, ReNew Power in collaboration with the Lucknow 
administration had setup a mask manufacturing unit supporting livelihoods for 200 SHG women in 
the district with an aim to manufacture 5 lac masks to be distributed to frontline workers in the state 
of Uttar Pradesh.  
In UP, ReNew Power is setting up a one of a kind “Floating Solar” power project of 100 MW capacity 
in G.B. Pant Sagar (Rihand Dam) in Sonbhadra District at an estimated cost of Rs 600 cr. ReNew’s 
Climate Connect Division has been working with the Discoms of UP for the past three years in 
demand forecasting, resulting in a substantial saving of nearly Rs 1,000 cr to the Discoms. 
 
As an employee centric organisation, ReNew Power has also taken  several proactive measures to 
preserve the well -being of its employees. It is getting employees, their families and support staff 
vaccinated. The company is providing 100% salary for the first three months and 50% of salary to the 
spouse or parents for th e next two years, in case of death of employee . It is also reimbursing 
children’s scholarship for education-tuition fee and providing education assistance for the spouse for

any educational course of the deceased. These initiatives are a new step in the im plementation of 
ReNew’s strategy to better align the benefits offered to its employees across the country. 
About ReNew Power:  ReNew Power is India’s leading renewable energy independent power 
producer by capacity and is the 13th largest global renewable IPP by operational capacity. ReNew 
develops, builds, owns, and operates utility-scale wind energy projects, utility-scale solar energy 
projects, utility-scale firm power projects and distributed solar energy projects. As of March 31, 
2021, ReNew Power had a total capacity of close to 10 GW of wind and solar energy projects across 
India, including commissioned and committed projects. ReNew has a strong track record of organic 
and inorganic growth. ReNew’s current group of stockholders contain several marquee investors 
including Goldman Sachs, CPP Investments, Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, GEF SACEF and JERA.
