ReNew Power spends 1.1 Cr towards COVID relief in 
Karnataka 
- 50 Oxygen Concentrators handed over to Karnataka State Medical Supplies Corporation Limited  
Bengaluru, India, June 28, 2021: ReNew Power Private Limited (“ReNew Power” or the “Company”), 
India’s leading renewable energy company today announced that it has undertaken relief work 
worth approximate Rs 1.1 Crore towards COVID relief in Karnataka. Representatives from ReNew 
Power met the Hon’ble CM, Mr. BS Yadiyurappa and briefed him about the various activities 
undertaken by the company in partnership with various state agencies for COVID relief.   
As part of its efforts to work with communities in the state, ReNew Power is donating 50 Oxygen 
Concentrators of 10 Litre capacity and PPE kits to the Karnataka State Medical Supplies Corporation 
Limited (KSMCL) for deployment in rural districts of the state immediately. The company will also 
donate 1 lac PPE kits for supporting the rural healthcare initiatives of the state.    
The company will also provision for 10 beds in Bidar and Gadag districts each, and assist the district 
hospitals at Davangere, Dharwad, Raichur and Yadgir with fully equipped ambulance besides 
supporting rural hospitals in Belgavi, Dharwad, Ballari, Tumkur, Gadag and Bagalkot with PPE Kits 
and sanitisers 
Speaking about the Initiative, Ms Vaishali Nigam Sinha, Chief Sustainability Officer, ReNew Power 
and Chair, ReNew Foundation said, “ReNew Power is committed to extending its support across the 
country to fight the pandemic. We are delighted to be a part of state governments efforts to 
augment and strengthen the healthcare infrastructure in the state and will continue to extend all 
necessary support to the state administration.” 
As an e mployee centric organisation, ReNew Power has also taken several proactive measures to 
preserve the well -being of its employees. It is getting emplo yees, their families and support staff 
vaccinated. ReNew Power’s CMD, Mr Sumant Sinha  recently announced that the company will 
provide 100% salary for the firs t t hree months and 50% of salary for th e next two yea rs, to the 
spouse or parents, of an employee who loses his life due to COVID. It is also reimbursing children’s 
scholarship for educatio n-tuition fee and providing education assistance to the spouse  of t he 
deceased.
