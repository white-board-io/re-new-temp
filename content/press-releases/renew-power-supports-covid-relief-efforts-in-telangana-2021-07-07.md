ReNew Power supports COVID relief efforts in Telangana 
Hyderabad, India, July 1, 2021: ReNew Power Private Limited (“ReNew Power” or the “Company”), 
India’s leading renewable energy company today announce that it will donate Oxygen concentrators, 
PPE Kits, ambulances, and hospital beds in the districts adjoining its facilities. The oxygen 
concentrators, ambulances and hospital beds will be provided in Dichipally & Minpur, while 
distribution of PPE Kits will be done across 6 districts in rural Telangana.  
These efforts are part of ReNew Power’s CSR initiative to help government hospitals combat COVID 
in various districts of the country. Besides supplying oxygen concentrators to the government 
hospitals, ReNew Power is also supplementing the efforts of various State Governments through 
donation of extra beds, ambulance support and distribution of PPE kits to frontline workers in 
hospitals. The Company has made similar contributions in Gujarat, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, 
Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar & Karnataka.  
Speaking about the initiative, Ms Vaishali Nigam Sinha, Chief Sustainability Officer, ReNew Power 
and Chair ReNew Foundation said, “ReNew Power is committed towards supporting the COVID 
relief measures in the state of Telangana by providing essential supplies to the local authorities, 
wherever necessary. Our teams on ground are working with the local authorities to ensure that the 
requisite supplies reach various parts of the state. Our endeavour is to strengthen the state 
government’s efforts in tackling the pandemic.” 
As an employee centric organisation, ReNew Power has also taken several proactive measures to 
preserve the well-being of its employees. It is getting employees, their families and support staff 
vaccinated. ReNew Power’s CMD, Mr Sumant Sinha recently announced that the company will 
provide 100% salary for the first three months and 50% of salary for the next two years, to the 
spouse, parents or children of an employee who loses his life due to COVID. It will also provide 
scholarship for children’s tuition fee and provide education assistance to the spouse of the 
deceased. 
About ReNew Power:  ReNew Power is India’s leading renewable energy independent power 
producer by capacity and is the 13th largest global renewable IPP by operational capacity. ReNew 
develops, builds, owns, and operates utility-scale wind energy projects, utility-scale solar energy 
projects, utility-scale firm power projects and distributed solar energy projects. As of March 31, 
2021, ReNew Power had a total capacity of close to 10 GW of wind and solar energy projects across 
India, including commissioned and committed projects. ReNew has a strong track record of organic 
and inorganic growth. ReNew’s current group of stockholders contain several marquee investors 
including Goldman Sachs, CPP Investments, Abu Dhabi Investment Authority, GEF SACEF and JERA.
