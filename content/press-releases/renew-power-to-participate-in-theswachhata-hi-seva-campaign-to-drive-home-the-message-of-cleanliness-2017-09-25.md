ReNew Power to participate in the ‘Swachhata Hi Seva’ campaign to  
drive home the message of cleanliness  
To reach over one lakh people in 80 villages 
New Delhi, September 25th, 2017:  ReNew Power Ventures (‘’ReNew Power’’), India’s leading and 
fastest growing clean energy company  announced its  participation in ‘Swatchhata Hi Seva’, an 
initiative of the Hon’ble Prime Minister of India to promote the message of cleanliness. 
ReNew Power through its ReNew India Initiative  (RII) is committed to build cleaner communities by 
ensuring that people in communities  across all its operational areas  are constantly educated on 
cleanliness and sanitation. The ‘Swachhata Hi Seva’ initiative, an extension of the Swa chh Bharat 
Abhiyan, which will be running from September 22, 2017 to October 2, 2017 will help to generate 
further awareness about the importance of cleanliness in over one lakh people in 80 villages in India. 
A key activity of RII as part of the ‘Swachhata hi Seva’ will be educating the current generation of 
school children on the importance of cleanliness, hygiene and sanitation. These children will then 
become ambassadors of change and will generate additional awareness among other residents both 
young and old of their villages on the impact of various elements of cleanliness. Apart from this, some 
of the other activities that are being carried out by RII as part of the ‘Swachhata Hi Seva’ initiative are:  
 Marches and awareness sessions in schools and community centres for the young residents, 
adults and women of the villages to facilitate discussions on the basics of hygiene and 
sanitation. 
 Cleanliness drives involving residents of the villages. 
 Cricket matches and marathons to be used as platforms for ‘Swachhata hi Seva’. 
 Placement of dustbins with social messaging at prominent and public places. 
Speaking about the campaign, Sumant Sinha, Founder Chairman and CEO, ReNew Power, says, ‘’As 
one of India’s leading clean energy companies, we are delighted to be a part of the ‘Swatchhata Hi 
Seva’ initiative. ReNew Power is constantly working with the local communities near its wind and solar 
projects to not just educate its residents on the benefits of cleanliness but also provides access to basic 
healthcare services, primary ed ucation and create  livelihood opportunities . This initi ative of our 
Hon’ble Prime Minister will help to provide  a fillip to the on -going efforts of RII to further build and 
enhance our human capital.” 
Programmes conducted under RII to enhance and build its  social, natural and huma n capital like  
women empowerment through the self -help group model, water conservation and the provisi on of 
clean drinking water and other on -going activities carry with them a large emphasis on cleanliness, 
hygiene and sanitation. As part of this campaign, ReNew Power will continue to work towards forging 
strong relationships to positively impact the lives of people in and around our operations by facilitating 
discussions to spread awareness about the positive impact of cleanliness.
