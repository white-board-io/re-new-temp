Press Release 
ReNew Power Ventures signs MOU with Government of Uttar Pradesh to invest Rs. 8,000 crores 
for 1,200 MW of renewable energy capacity 
- MOU could create employment opportunities for 12,000 people in the state 
 
New Delhi/Mumbai, 21 February 2018: ReNew Power, one of India’s leading clean energy companies, 
has signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the Government of Uttar Pradesh today to 
facilitate development of 1200 MW of renewable energy installed cap acity in the state.  An investment of 
INR 8000 crores is proposed to commission these projects which are slated to help the state meet its energy 
needs. The MoU was signed as part of ‘UP Investors Summit 2018’, in the presence of Honorable Chief 
Minister of Uttar Pradesh Shri Yogi Adityanath.  
 
The company’ proposal, to develop 1000 MW of installed capacity for solar power projects alongside 
another 200 MW for waste-to-energy projects, is in line with the India’s goal to provide access to energy for 
all through clean energy. These projects have the potential to create direct employment for 5,500 people 
and indirect employment for 6,500 people in the state.  
Talking about the investment, Sumant Sinha, CEO, ReNew Power said, “ReNew Power is a proud partner 
in India’s clean energy journey. We are committed to helping the vast and resourceful state of Uttar Pradesh 
reach its full potential by providing clean and innovative energy solutions. There is a significant potential for 
generating solar power in the state. Technology, entrepreneurship and innovation has ensured that the cost 
of solar power is on the declining trend as compared to the cost of fossil fuel -based electricity. Therefore, 
solar and other renewable energy investments are critical in helping the st ates to achieve their energy 
targets.” 
 
The proposed investment comes in the wake of a national effort to increase India’s renewable capacity to 
175 GW by 2022, bridging the expected demand-supply gap in the coming years.
