Vidya School to generate power through solar 
energy  
 
Gurugram, India, August 26, 2019: A 51.2 kW solar -rooftop system  was inaugurated  at Vidya  School, 
Gurugram, today. The system was  installed by ReNew Power, India’s leading  renewable energy company. 
The project, undertaken by ReNew as a part of its ‘Lighting Lives’ CSR initiative, will allow Vidya School to 
generate electricity for its own consumption, using solar radiation. 
 
The solar rooftop installation will provide electricity to the school comp lex and will help Vidya School  save 
Rs 5 lakh every year in electricity bills. The school plans to use the savings for scholarships and financial 
assistance to its students. Vidya S chool provides education to around 1100 students from the 
underprivileged sections of the society.   
 
The ‘Lighting Lives’ initiative is one of the flagship CSR programmes of ReNew Power, aimed at providing 
electricity solutions free of cost and through clean energy technology, to educational institutions across the 
country. 
 
Commenting on the ‘Lighting L ives’ initiative Vaishali Nigam Sinha, Chief Sustainability, CSR and 
Communication officer said: “We are proud to do our bit in helping transform energy usage through access 
to clean energy. Through this project, not only is there a positive impact on the environment due to carbon 
emission mitigation, but the young students of the school are also sensitized towards issues of climate 
change, and are propelled to become active ambassadors of clean energy and sustainability”.  
 
After the inauguration of the rooftop solar plant students of Vidya School took a pledge on following a 
sustainable lifestyle. 
 
Talking about the initiative, Rashmi Misra founder of Vidya  School said: “We are delighted at this 
partnership with ReNew Power and we expect that this is another step forward in becoming an environment 
friendly school. A Green school is a school that creates a healthy environment, conducive to learning, while 
saving energy, environmental resources and money.”  
 
ReNew has already electrified 21 government schools across the country, which were not even getting 2 
hours of electricity. All the schools have been electrified using solar energy. ReNew is looking to expand the 
initiative to light up 500 schools over the next 10 years across India and is partnering with various state 
governments to light up schools in those areas which are power deficient. 
 
About Vidya School  
VIDYA with its remarkable journey of 33 years is a registered not -for-profit organization working for the education, 
empowerment, and transformation of less privileged children, youth and women through micro -level intervention. 
VIDYA was founded in 1985 by  Rashmi Misra, who taught a class of five girls at her home on the campus of IIT Delhi, 
which turned out to be the start of a movement. In the past three decades, VIDYA has impacted over 3,75,000 people 
with a presence in 5 metropolitan cities (Delhi, Gurg aon, Bangalore, Mumbai &amp; Pune) and 78 successful projects 
across the country.  VIDYA's underlying philosophy is to build the leaders and responsible citizens of tomorrow  while 
believing that less -privileged students should have access to the same qualit y of education available at the country’s 
best schools, The VIDYA School, Gurugram was established in 2009  with a vision to provide 1000+ underprivileged 
children high-quality education, with the infrastructure and  facilities on par with some of the best p ublic schools in the 
city. The award-winning CBSE accredited K-12 school is an English -medium school with IT -enabled classrooms, well -
equipped libraries, a robotics lab, an English Language digital learning lab, and sports facilities offer  education and 
facilities on par with the nation’s most elite schools."

About ReNew Power

scale wind and solar energy projects as well as distributed solar energy projects that generate energy for commercial 
and industrial customers. ReNew has a strong track record of organic and inorganic growth having nearly doubled its
